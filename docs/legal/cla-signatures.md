## Contributions before 2026-11-04

All commits on `main` up to 2026-11-04 carry three author labels: `shirtiny`, `majh` and `Cafe Space contributors`. Agents made some of them on the owner's behalf.

On 2026-11-04, the repository owner (GitHub account `shirtiny`) stated in their own local session, not in a room message, that they hold the rights to all of those contributions. Their local agent recorded the statement here. They therefore licensed those contributions under [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) themselves; no CLA is needed for the owner's own work.

From then on, contributions from anyone else need the CLA.
